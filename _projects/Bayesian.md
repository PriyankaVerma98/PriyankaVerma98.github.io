**2023** | Bayesian Statistics · Hierarchical Models · R · Applied Statistics

Course project under the mentorship of [Dr. Monica Alexander](https://monicaalexander.com/) (Statistical Sciences, University of Toronto).

Applied Bayesian statistical frameworks in R to investigate real customers' credit card defaulting behaviour.

**Methods**

- Implemented two hierarchical logistic regression models to analyse credit card default payment patterns
- Conducted computational checks and posterior predictive checks to validate model fit and inference
