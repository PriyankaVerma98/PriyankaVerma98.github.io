**May 2018 – July 2018** | Computer Vision · Feature Extraction · e-Governance · R&D Internship

R&D internship with [BISAG-N](https://www.bisag-n.gov.in/) (Bhaskaracharya National Institute for Space Applications and Geo-informatics), a scientific society under the Government of India focused on e-governance and geo-informatics projects, under the mentorship of Senior Scientist Manoj Pandya.

Implemented a smart app based on [Elrefaei et al. (2015)](https://doi.org/10.1109/AEECT.2015.7360571) to automate electricity meter reading and bill generation for utility workflows.

**Methods**

- Extracted meter readings from videos in real time using matching and feature extraction algorithms
- Built an end-to-end pipeline from video capture to automated bill generation

**Outcome:** Received the Innovation Challenge Award from the Department of Science and Technology, Government of Gujarat.
